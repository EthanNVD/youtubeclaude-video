# Outline — Why Operation Market Garden Was COMPLETELY Avoidable (34:04, EN)

**Question:** Was the defeat at Arnhem (the last bridge) avoidable?
**Verdict:** Yes — not by one heroic decision: 5 Allied exits (Antwerp, one lift a day, distant drop zones, Bedell Smith's warning, Nijmegen bridge on day one), 2 German ones (Grave, Nijmegen left standing), Lent left blank. Any one Allied exit might have been enough; they took none. The SS at Arnhem was luck, not treason.

**Device:** THE ROAD — Hell's Highway drawn as one road (Belgian border → Eindhoven → Son → Grave → Nijmegen → Lent → Elst → Arnhem) with EXIT signs. Each missed exit gets painted: Allied (blue, numbered A1–A5) or German (grey, G1–G2); "time" exits get NO SIGN; Lent stays BLANK (white). A two-column dispatcher's LEDGER (Allied / German) counts the signs. THE CLOCK: timetable "ARNHEM: DAY 2–3" vs actual elapsed time (badge).
The **host** = "Sir", the dispatcher. His **aide** = a young airborne signaller with a Type 22 set (5 km range) who asks the questions ("Sir, it's one road?").
