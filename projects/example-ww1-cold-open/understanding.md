## 1. The film in three lines
- **Question:** When did Germany really lose the First World War?
- **Answer / verdict:** Not in the summer of 1918. The film weighs the earlier candidates: the Marne, the blockade, Verdun, Jutland, Pless, Amiens.
- **Era and sides:** WW1; Germany vs France and Britain.

- **Big questions (captions):**
  - "So how does … in a single summer?" (accents: Paris, collapse);
